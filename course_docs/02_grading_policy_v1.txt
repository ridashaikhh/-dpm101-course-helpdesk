1. PASSING CRITERIA (JANUARY 2026 COHORT)
  - To earn the Verified Certificate, learners must achieve an overall weighted score
    of at least 50% across quizzes, the capstone case, and the final exam.
  - Learners scoring 85% or above receive a "Pass with Distinction" badge.

2. LATE SUBMISSION POLICY (JANUARY 2026 COHORT)
  - Learners are allowed up to 2 late submissions across the course (applicable to
    weekly quizzes or the capstone case) up to 48 hours past the deadline.
  - A flat 10% mark deduction applies to any late submission submitted within that
    48-hour window.

3. CAPSTONE DEADLINE
  - The Capstone Product Case is due at the end of Week 7 (10 November 2026, 11:59 PM IST).
