1. ABOUT THE COURSE
DPM-101 is an 8-week online course covering user research, product roadmapping,
metrics, and AI feature design.

2. WEEKLY SCHEDULE & EFFORT
  - Duration: 8 weeks (self-paced video lectures + live weekend doubt sessions)
  - Expected Effort: 4 to 5 hours per week
  - Discussion Forum: Open 24/7. Teaching assistants reply within 24 hours.

3. ASSESSMENT WEIGHTS
  - Weekly Quizzes (6 quizzes, best 5 counted):   30% of total score
  - Capstone Product Case Submission:             30% of total score
  - Proctored Final Exam:                         40% of total score

4. CERTIFICATE TRACKS & FEE
  - Audit Track (Free): Access to all lecture videos and reading material. Does not
    include graded assignments, the final exam, or a certificate.
  - Verified Certificate Track (Rs. 1,500): Full access to graded quizzes, capstone
    evaluation, the proctored final exam, and the official Verified Certificate upon
    passing.
  - Upgrading from the Audit Track to the Verified Track is open until the end of Week 4.
