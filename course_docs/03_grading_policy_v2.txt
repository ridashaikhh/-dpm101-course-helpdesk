IMPORTANT UPDATE NOTICE:
This document supersedes "02_grading_policy_v1.txt". The academic committee updated the
passing threshold and late submission rules effective October 2026.

1. UPDATED PASSING CRITERIA (CURRENT COHORT)
  - To earn the Verified Certificate, learners must achieve an overall weighted score
    of at least 60% (increased from the earlier 50% threshold), AND must score at least
    40% in the Proctored Final Exam separately.
  - Learners scoring 85% or above still receive a "Pass with Distinction" badge.

2. UPDATED LATE SUBMISSION POLICY (CURRENT COHORT)
  - Late submissions are NO LONGER accepted for weekly quizzes or the capstone case.
    The earlier 48-hour grace window with a 10% penalty has been discontinued.
  - Because the best 5 out of 6 weekly quizzes are counted, learners can miss 1 weekly
    quiz without penalty.

3. CAPSTONE DEADLINE
  - The Capstone Product Case deadline has been extended by 5 days to 15 November 2026,
    11:59 PM IST.
